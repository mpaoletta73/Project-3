Project Write-Up

Research Question

How does El Niño alter key environmental indicators — sea surface temperature, precipitation, vegetation cover, and atmospheric conditions — across the Pacific region, as observed in NASA satellite and NOAA climate data?

Data Sources

  • NOAA Oceanic Niño Index (ONI): 3-month rolling average SST anomalies in the Niño 3.4 region (5°N–5°S, 170°W–120°W), 1950–present. CPC NCEP
  • NASA MODIS: Global daily satellite imagery (vegetation, land temperature, aerosols) via NASA Earthdata Worldview and the GIBS REST API.
  • NOAA GOES: Geostationary satellite imagery (GOES-16/17) accessed via AWS Open Data Registry.
  • CMIP6: Global climate model simulations (temperature, precipitation, greenhouse gas projections) from the NOAA public CMIP6 dataset on Google Cloud.
